Operational rules

Daily sequence

Record one temperature per patient per day, then complete the doctor's visit. The dashboard highlights missing work and blocks duplicate same-day readings.

Discharge readiness

A patient is flagged only after 3 full consecutive days with recorded temperatures below 38.0°C. Administrator confirms discharge; doctor documents clinical review.
